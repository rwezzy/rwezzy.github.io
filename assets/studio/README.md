# Studio desk props

- `laptop-screen.png` is the image supplied by the user for the laptop display. It is copied without modification from `codex-clipboard-eaddd7d7-5301-41d1-8eeb-bc5d86e833c8.png`. SHA-256: `8d52e24de4eea57cd60fbb5bc95f6c6e02fe6e83cafbf06d69090cf66711c328`. It shows Enya/music and a circuit reference on the left, with code on the right. The third-party app interface and artwork remain part of that user-supplied screen image; no separate license is asserted.
- The laptop enclosure is original procedural geometry inspired by the dark OMEN 16 form factor. It is an illustration, not HP CAD or a dimensional product model.
- The SoundScope prop reuses `models/soundscope/carrier-v3.glb` through the existing `loadCarrier` function. Its 18 printed parts are Keith Rwezahura's original project CAD. The tripod, camera envelope and populated microphone board are explanatory procedural geometry. They are not manufacturer CAD, and the component placement does not claim an exact electrical layout.
- The local manufacturer files under `tmp/portfolio_cad_assets/private_reference` and `tmp/manufacturer_cad` are not included. Their accompanying provenance file states that no redistribution license was supplied. Seeed's [official XVF3800 documentation](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/respeaker_xvf3800_introduction/) links mechanical downloads; its [license page](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/License/) addresses documents/images and software without explicitly identifying those CAD exports as covered. Redistribution permission for the local reference exports was therefore not established.
- The frame and top-bound notebook are original procedural geometry. No portrait was present in the project's `images` directory when these props were added. The frame therefore displays a name card rather than an invented person. `createStudioProps({ portraitUrl })` supports a supplied real portrait and preserves its proportions with a centered crop.

`js/studio-props.js` owns and disposes its geometry, materials and textures. It has no independent render loop or event listeners. Its About and Contact hit targets are handled by the studio scene; the host animates `setNotebookOpen(t)` and camera movement.
